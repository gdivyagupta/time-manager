Profile design

The profile is intentionally generic. Future UI versions can turn each section into normal forms without changing the underlying model.

  • Goals: what the system is optimising for and by when.
  • Sleep: minimum protected recovery.
  • Energy: when deep, light, and recovery tasks belong.
  • Categories: how time is classified.
  • Fixed commitments: recurring blocks that constrain planning.
  • Recurring responsibilities: weekly obligations without necessarily having a fixed clock time.
  • Weekly budgets: intentional allocation targets rather than mandatory quotas.
